The volume slider uses a custom curve instead of increasing volume 
evenly (linear). The first 75% of the slider controls volume from 
0% to 50%, making lower volume adjustments more gradual and precise. 
The remaining 25% of the 1000 slider steps controls volume from 50% 
to 100%, allowing the volume to increase much faster near the top. 
The two formulas let the player convert between slider position and 
volume percentage in either direction. it is for the ability to get 
more specifc lower volume values. I keep it at 30% so my system wide 
can be loud, but my. audio my not be.

VOLUME CURVE FORMULAS

SLIDER TO VOLUME:

t = clamp(p, 0, 1000) / 1000

V(t) =
  (200 / 3) * t,  if 0 <= t <= 0.75
  200 * t - 100,  if 0.75 < t <= 1

Volume = round(V(t))


VOLUME TO SLIDER (INVERSE):

V = clamp(volume, 0, 100)

p(V) =
  15 * V,       if 0 <= V <= 50
  5 * V + 500,  if 50 < V <= 100

Slider Position = round(p(V))


VARIABLES:

p = slider position (0–1000)
t = normalized slider position (0–1)
V = volume percentage (0–100)
clamp = restricts a value to the specified range
round = rounds to the nearest integer


BEHAVIOR:

- 0% volume = slider position 0
- 50% volume = slider position 750
- 100% volume = slider position 1000
- The first 75% of slider travel controls 0–50% volume.
- The final 25% of slider travel controls 50–100% volume.
- Both conversion functions clamp inputs to their valid ranges.
- Both functions round their final results to the nearest integer.
